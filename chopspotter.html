Detection Pipeline

From sound wave
to SMS in seconds

A five-microphone array listens continuously for the acoustic signature of a cutting tool engaged with a metal exhaust pipe. No cameras. No line of sight required.

01

Capture

A five-element microphone array captures ambient audio from the monitored vehicle area at 22,050 Hz, with beamforming focused toward the protected zone.

›
02

Separate

Proprietary triple-pass harmonic separation isolates the sustained blade-vibration harmonics of cutting tools from background environmental noise.

›
03

Classify

An on-device Spectral-Classification network processes the acoustic signal in real time, identifying angle grinders, reciprocating saws, pipe cutters, and hacksaws.

›
04

Alert

A Bayesian evidence accumulator suppresses false positives before dispatching an SMS, email, and cloud event record the moment a genuine theft event is confirmed.

Capabilities

Built for lots,
dealerships, and fleets

ChopSpotter is engineered for real-world commercial deployments — not laboratory conditions. It works where cameras can't see and alarms don't reach.

Real-Time Detection

Continuous acoustic monitoring with on-device AI inference. No cloud round-trip required for detection — alerts dispatch within seconds of event confirmation.

No Line of Sight

Sound travels through and around vehicles. ChopSpotter monitors an entire parking area from a single mounting point — including vehicles obscured from camera views.

Instant SMS and Email Alerts

Alerts reach your phone and inbox before the thief has finished cutting. Include the time, location, and event classification in every notification.

Low False-Positive Rate

A Bayesian evidence accumulator requires sustained classification confidence across multiple analysis windows before dispatching an alert — ignoring car doors, thunder, and passing aircraft.

Cellular Failover

WiFi is the primary alert path. A built-in cellular backup ensures alerts reach you even if the local network is disrupted — including by the thieves themselves.

Battery or Hardwired

Operates from a hardwired DC supply or a rechargeable LiPo battery, enabling deployment at any location in a parking area without dedicated power infrastructure.

Under the hood

Edge AI, no cloud dependency

All signal processing and classification runs locally on the device. ChopSpotter does not depend on a cloud server to make a detection decision — only to deliver the alert.
